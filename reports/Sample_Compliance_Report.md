# EU Compliance Assessment Report: Mandatory Risk Management Requirements for High-Risk AI Systems under the EU AI Act

## Introduction
This report outlines the mandatory risk management requirements applicable to high-risk AI systems as stipulated in the EU AI Act (CELEX: 32024R1689). The requirements are designed to ensure the safety, health, and fundamental rights of individuals and to promote trustworthiness in AI technologies.

## Key Mandatory Requirements

### 1. Risk Management System
- **Establishment of a Risk Management System**: Providers of high-risk AI systems must establish a risk management system that considers the intended purpose and context of use of the AI system (Recital 64).
  
### 2. Data Governance and Management
- **Quality and Relevance of Data Sets**: High-quality data sets for training, validation, and testing must be relevant, sufficiently representative, and as free from bias as possible. This is crucial to ensure that the AI system performs as intended and does not lead to discrimination (Recital 67).

### 3. Technical Documentation and Record-Keeping
- **Documentation Requirements**: Providers must maintain comprehensive technical documentation and records that demonstrate compliance with the mandatory requirements (Recital 66).

### 4. Transparency and Information Provision
- **Transparency Obligations**: Providers must ensure transparency in the functioning of the AI system and provide necessary information to deployers (Recital 66).

### 5. Human Oversight
- **Human Oversight Mechanisms**: High-risk AI systems must incorporate mechanisms for human oversight to mitigate risks effectively (Recital 66).

### 6. Robustness, Accuracy, and Cybersecurity
- **Ensuring Robustness and Accuracy**: Providers must ensure that the AI system is robust and accurate, with measures in place to address potential cybersecurity threats (Recital 66).

### 7. Compliance with Additional Legal Frameworks
- **Harmonisation with Other Legislation**: Compliance with other applicable EU harmonisation legislation is required, as the availability or service of the AI system can only occur when it meets all relevant legal standards (Recital 64).

## Information Submission Requirements
### For Providers of High-Risk AI Systems (Article 6, Section B)
- Name, address, and contact details of the provider.
- Description of the intended purpose of the AI system.
- Identification and traceability references for the AI system.
- Status of the AI system (market availability, in service, etc.).

### For Deployers of High-Risk AI Systems (Article 6, Section C)
- Name, address, and contact details of the deployer.
- Summary of findings from the fundamental rights impact assessment (Article 27).
- Summary of the data protection impact assessment (Article 35 of Regulation (EU) 2016/679).

## Key Takeaways
- High-risk AI systems are subject to stringent risk management requirements to ensure compliance with health, safety, and fundamental rights.
- Providers must maintain high standards of data governance and technical documentation.
- Transparency and human oversight are critical components of the risk management framework.
- Compliance with other EU legislation is mandatory for the deployment of high-risk AI systems.
